About the shop

My father started with one kadhai and one stove

I am Ramesh Sharma. In 1989, my father Late Shri Gopal Sharma began selling laddoos from a small wooden counter in this same lane. I still remember stirring the sugar syrup beside him before school.

Today my son Ankit helps me at the counter, but the recipes have not changed. We use the same ghee supplier, the same slow roasting and the same early morning start.

When you buy from us, you are buying from a neighbour, not a brand.

  • Pure desi ghee only, no vanaspati
  • Made fresh every morning, never stored for days
  • Less sugar on request
  • Free home delivery within 3 km on orders above ₹500
  • Bulk orders taken 2 days ahead
